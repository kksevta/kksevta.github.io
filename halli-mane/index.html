The name says it all

Food that tastes like a village kitchen.

Out on the edge of Bengaluru, past Varthur, Halli Mane serves the kind of meals you'd get at a family home: simple, filling and full of flavour. Veg and non-veg, breakfast to dinner, every day of the week.
